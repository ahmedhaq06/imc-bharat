Membership Tiers & Roles

Find Your Place in the Count.

Six ways to be part of India's newest community operating system. Every seat matters. Find yours.

The Six Membership Tiers

🌱 Tier I

Observer

Be Counted. Belong.

The simplest, most direct way to join the IMC network. Register yourself, get counted in your PIN code, and access the community platform and local listings. Open registration — no invite needed.

✅ Tier II

Verified Member

In this network, your word travels with you.

Verification at IMC means someone already inside the network knows you and stands by your membership. Access full community platform and local listings within your PIN code.

🎓 Tier III

Campus Member

Your Campus. Your Count.

For college and university students. Verified through your College or School ID, Campus Members get access to the IMC network, Talent Bank, and job postings right from the start of their careers.

💼 Tier IV

Professional Member

Your Profession. Your Community's Asset.

For individual professionals — doctors, lawyers, engineers, educators, accountants, influencers, entrepreneurs. Be listed as a trusted professional within your PIN code network.

🏛️ Tier V

Contributor Member

Organisations That Count.

For NGOs, corporates, companies, hospitals and institutions committed to serving the community. Your organisation gets listed, connected and recognised within the network.

📍 Tier VI

PIN Code Pioneer

One Per PIN. That Person Is You.

The most responsible role in the IMC network. As sole Pioneer for your PIN code, you anchor local trust with access to member contact details within your locality to enable real action.
