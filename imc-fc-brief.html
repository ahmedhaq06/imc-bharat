Our Vision
"Every Indian Muslim — counted, connected, and supported — by their own community."
Why IMC Exists
India's Muslim community is the second largest in the world — yet remains one of the least organised. There is no verified directory of who we are, where we live, or what we can offer each other. No trusted platform to find help, offer a skill, or mobilise in a crisis.

IMC changes that. We are building a verified, PIN code–based member network — a living directory of Indian Muslims, connected by community, protected by trust.
