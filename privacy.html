Welcome to the IMC (Indian Muslim Community) platform operated by [IMC LEGAL ENTITY NAME]. We build digital community infrastructure connecting verified members, professionals, organisations, and opportunities across Indian PIN codes. Your privacy, personal autonomy, and trust are fundamental to our mission. This policy outlines how your digital personal data is processed, stored, and protected in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India.

01.

Scope & Applicable Legal Framework

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data collected through the IMC website, mobile applications, PIN code lookup directory, member verification portals, and community services (collectively, the "Platform").

The Platform operates under the statutory data protection framework of the Republic of India, primarily governed by:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act, 2023") and applicable DPDP Rules.
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 ("IT Act") and Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • Applicable rules enacted by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the Data Protection Board of India.

By accessing or creating an account on the Platform, you acknowledge that your personal data will be processed in accordance with the terms of this Policy.

02.

Personal Data We Collect

IMC practices strict data minimization. We only collect personal data necessary to enable community connection, local discovery, and account verification:

A. Directly Provided Data

  • Registration Information: Full name, primary contact number (WhatsApp/SMS), email address, preferred language.
  • Location Identifiers: State, City/District, Ward/Locality, and 6-digit Indian Postal Index Number (PIN Code).
  • Profile Details: Professional designation, industry domain, education credentials, organization affiliation, and voluntary bio.
  • Verification Credentials: Optional identity verification proofs (e.g., professional degree, institutional ID, council registration) submitted voluntarily for verified member badges.
  • Communication Records: Feedback forms, support requests, and grievance communications.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & Access Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, operating system version.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymized map interaction events, PIN code search queries, section dwell times, and navigation logs.
03.

How Data Is Collected

We collect data through the following transparent channels:

  • Direct User Submissions: Forms filled during registration, profile setup, PIN code exploration, or Founding Council applications.
  • Interactive Feature Inputs: City and PIN code lookups performed on our interactive community discovery map.
  • Automated Telemetry: Standard web server access logs and essential cookie session tokens required for platform security.

We do not acquire member databases from third-party data brokers, nor do we perform hidden device tracking.

04.

Purpose & Processing Basis

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, IMC processes personal data strictly based on specified, legitimate grounds:

  • Community Connection & Local Discovery: Connecting members with verified peers, professional mentors, and emergency support near their PIN code.
  • Verification & Trust Maintenance: Preventing fraudulent profiles and establishing community trust credentials.
  • Service Communications: Sending platform notifications, verification updates, and security alerts via SMS/WhatsApp/Email.
  • Legal & Compliance Obligations: Responding to lawful orders by authorized enforcement authorities under Indian law.
05.

Consent & Consent Withdrawal

Processing of personal data is initiated only after obtaining explicit, affirmative, and informed consent.

Notice & Regional Languages

In compliance with the DPDP Act 2023, consent notices are available in clear English and will be accessible in all 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India as platform localization rolls out.

Right to Withdraw Consent:

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Consent withdrawal can be initiated via profile privacy settings or by writing to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. Upon withdrawal, IMC will cease processing your personal data within 72 hours, except where retention is legally mandated.

06.

Member Profiles & Visibility

IMC gives you full control over how your profile information appears across the community directory:

  • Public View: Non-members and general web traffic can only see aggregated PIN code member counts and anonymized city nodes.
  • Verified Member Directory: Detailed professional credentials are visible only to verified logged-in community members.
  • Phone & Contact Masking: Personal phone numbers and email addresses are hidden by default and require mutual consent for connection requests.
07.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

Data is shared strictly under confidential Data Processing Agreements with trusted service providers:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure ISO-certified data center hosts operating within India.
  • Communication Gateways: Verified SMS/WhatsApp API gateways strictly for dispatching OTPs and account notifications.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosed only when mandated by official legal warrants issued under Indian law.
08.

Data Retention & Storage

Personal data is retained only for the duration required to fulfill community purposes or statutory compliance:

  • Active Membership: Data is retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Account Deactivation: Upon account closure request, profile data is purged from active databases within 30 days.
  • Compliance Archival: Certain transaction and audit logs may be retained for statutory periods mandated under Indian tax and IT rules.
09.

Data Security Safeguards

IMC implements industry-standard technical, operational, and organizational security measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols across all web and API endpoints.
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 database encryption for sensitive verification credentials.
  • Access Control: Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) preventing unauthorized internal data access.
  • Security Audits: Periodic vulnerability assessments and web application firewall monitoring.
10.

Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

As a Data Principal under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, you enjoy the following enforceable statutory rights:

  • Right to Summary Information: Access a clear summary of personal data held by IMC and processing activities.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate data or complete erasure of personal records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Right to file complaints with our Grievance Officer and subsequently the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to Nominate: Right to nominate an individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, write to our Data Protection Desk at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

11.

Children's & Minors' Data Policy

In accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, IMC does not knowingly process personal data of individuals under 18 years of age without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian. We do not engage in behavioral tracking or targeted messaging aimed at minors.

12.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential session tokens and local browser storage strictly for:

  • Maintaining authentication state across sessions.
  • Caching location map view preferences for fast map rendering.

We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising cookies.

13.

Security Incident & Breach Handling

In the event of a verified personal data breach, IMC maintains a response protocol to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals without undue delay, outlining the nature of the breach and corrective measures taken.
